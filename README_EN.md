# Real Estate Ad Design Assistant

A design Skill for real-estate marketing and property-service visuals. It turns project content, real source assets and revision feedback into executable decisions for composition, typography, layout, color, material, IP characters and image–type relationships, and produces integrated artwork when the available tools support it.

The goal is not to apply a preset style. It first decides what the audience should remember, then builds a visual relationship that fits the project, medium and communication task.

## What it supports

- Brand and service-system launches, project naming, luxury positioning, cultural value and brand statements.
- Architecture, landscape, product value, sales milestones, delivery service and source-asset integration.
- Community festivals, IP-led activities, awards, notices, location graphics and campaign series.
- Concept directions, integrated first drafts, targeted revisions, and handoffs for designers or image tools.

Production and precision-editing capabilities depend on the host environment.

## Latest design workflow

### A/B/C direction selection

When the user asks to “follow the process,” the Skill condenses the brief, presents three meaningfully different directions, recommends one, and pauses for selection before production. If the user requests an immediate draft or has already selected a direction, it proceeds without repeating the proposal stage.

### Default role of refined low-intervention design

A full-field, low-intervention approach normally appears as direction B. For brand launches, cultural value, luxury imagery, brand statements and project-name reveals, it becomes the default direction A. It is not a fixed beige, black-gold or clay template: photography, a single color field, stone, textile, metal, embossing, seams or continuous light can carry the main visual weight.

### Stable center without mirror symmetry

Formal real-estate key visuals usually test a stable information center with a consistent visual language on both sides. The headline may sit on or near the center axis while material, curves, light, color range and detail density echo across the frame without being mirrored. A side-aligned layout can lead when architecture, a person, product, landscape or directional light provides a clear counterweight.

### Typography, layout and IP

The workflow resolves hierarchy, semantic line breaks, type-block scale, alignment and optical centering before choosing letterform details, decoration or material effects. IP characters are designed through communication role, identity core, action, props, environmental contact and relationship to type—not added as corner stickers.

### Time as a viewing relationship

Temporal ideas such as continuity, future, growth, companionship, renewal and arrival are first classified as moment, cycle, process, accumulation or generation. They may be expressed through changing light, layered material, progressive paths, temperature shifts or old/new evidence. Clocks, calendars, hourglasses and light trails are optional, not defaults.

## Knowledge base

- 42 original design studies across festivals and IP, brand and commercial work, real assets, sales milestones, product value, seasonal communities and campaign formats.
- Close reading of 45 positive user references, grouped into transferable method families, plus a dedicated typography and layout study of the same 45 works.
- Blue cartoon oceans, floral pink haze and stone-plus-metal trim are treated as valid positive paths; media are judged by relationships and execution rather than element names.
- Documented 2025–2026 observations with source and date distinctions, representative visual analysis and real-estate adaptation boundaries.
- Guidance for image–type structure, color and material, contemporary aesthetics, real assets, IP, production, revision and visual QA.

References transfer design relationships only. They do not copy project brands, wording, IP, proprietary lettering or complete compositions, and third-party campaign images are not bundled.

## Principles

Watercolor, photography, opaque painting, 3D and flat graphics; beige, red-gold, black-gold and bright color; people, objects, buildings and art installations are equal candidates. Selection depends on the current content, assets, reading experience and production conditions—not whether a medium is common, previously unsuccessful or recently reused.

User and medium requirements determine the canvas. When unspecified, a key visual may begin at 16:9, a mobile poster at 9:16 and a Xiaohongshu post at 3:4. Official logos, buildings, maps, awards, numbers and IP are checked against real assets.
